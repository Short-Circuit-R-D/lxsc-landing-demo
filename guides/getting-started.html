Your first study, step by step

Six steps from sign-in to a report. Studio asks one thing at a time, so you can follow along.

  • Sign in
  • Start a project
  • Add the room
  • Set targets
  • Place fixtures
  • Read and report

  1. Sign in

    Studio needs an account. Sign in with your LuxScale account, then open Projects at studio.lxscale.com.

  2. Start a project

    Choose NEW PROJECT to start blank, WIZARD to be guided with questions, or IMPORT to open a study you saved earlier.

  3. Add the room

    Upload a DWG or DXF, check the rooms Studio detects, and confirm the unit scale. No drawing? Draw the shape or enter its dimensions by hand.

    Need more rooms? Use ADD ROOM.

  4. Set the room and the targets

    Enter the ceiling height and the reflectances of the ceiling, walls and floor, plus the maintenance factor. Set the task plane height and its boundary margin.

    Pick the EN 12464-1 activity and the targets fill in. Designing to your own brief? Set the targets yourself.

  5. Place the fixtures

    Upload your IES or LDT files, set the mounting height for each fixture, and place them by hand. Or press AUTOMATE and let the engine propose a layout.

    Then press CALCULATE ROOM.

  6. Read the result and export

    The results panel shows average, minimum and maximum illuminance, uniformity (U₀) and power density for each task plane. Open the REPORT tab to see the full report, then EXPORT it as a PDF. Press SAVE to keep your work.

What the statuses mean

PassedAverage illuminance and uniformity meet the targets.
FailedA target is missed. The message tells you which one and by how much.
Not recommended (overcapped)The target is met, but the average is above the overdesign cap, so you are using more light than needed.
UnconstrainedCalculated without targets, so there is nothing to pass or fail.

Not sure where to start? Press Wizard. It asks about your room, the standard and the heights, then builds the study with you.
