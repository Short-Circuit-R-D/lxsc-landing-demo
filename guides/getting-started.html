Your first lighting study in seven steps

Follow the path from sign-in to a PDF report. The Quickstart wizard can do the setup with you, or you can build everything by hand.

Before you begin

A desktop or laptop screenStudio and the report view need a display at least 1024 px wide. On a narrower screen you will see Desktop Screen Required.
A Short Circuit accountYou sign in with Short Circuit single sign-on, which saves your projects to your workspace.
Save as you goStudio does not autosave. A dot on the SAVE button tells you there are unsaved changes.

The seven steps

  1. Sign in

    Open Studio and click Sign in with Short Circuit. After the single sign-on prompt you land on the Projects dashboard, where you can search, sort, copy, import and export projects.

  2. Start a project

    Click WIZARD for the guided five-step setup, NEW PROJECT for a blank canvas with an empty Room 01, or IMPORT to open a .lxsc or .lxsc.zip archive.

    You can reopen the wizard at any time inside Studio with RUN WIZARD.

  3. Define the room

    In the wizard, enter length, width and ceiling height, type vertex coordinates for an L-shaped room, or select a room from a CAD drawing. On a blank canvas, open Room Architecture and trace the walls with ORTHO 90° on, or use IMPORT FROM CAD.

    Need more rooms? Use ADD ROOM in the room bar.

  4. Set the workplane, target and physics

    Set the workplane height and the wall zone. Choose a standard activity, a custom target or none. Then check the surface reflectances and the maintenance factor.

    The wizard starts with a 0.80 m workplane, a 0.50 m wall zone, reflectances of 0.7, 0.5 and 0.2 for ceiling, walls and floor, and a maintenance factor of 0.80.

  5. Add luminaires

    Open Fixture Catalog, choose a luminaire from the SC Catalog or upload your own .ies or .ldt file, then click inside the room to place it. For faster layouts, open Photometric Layout and draw a Grid, Staggered, Boundary or Linear pattern.

    Prefer a suggestion? Set a target and press AUTOMATE to test catalog luminaires and grids. The wizard offers the same through Run Automated AI Study.

  6. Calculate and read the results

    Press CALCULATE ROOM or hit Enter. The right sidebar shows maximum and minimum illuminance, uniformity and power density, with a compliance badge for each workplane. Switch on FALSE COLOR and the LUX PROBE to see where the light falls.

    If the button is grayed out, hover over it. The tooltip names the missing room, luminaire, workplane or height problem.

  7. Save and export the report

    Name the project and click SAVE. Switch to Report, fill in Page Setup, the project overview, an optional signature and the rooms and solutions to include, then click Export PDF and choose Save as PDF.

What the results mean

PassAverage illuminance and uniformity meet the target without exceeding the overdesign cap. The report calls this Passed.
Not recommendedThe target is met, but the average is above the overdesign cap, so you are using more light than needed.
Non-compliantA target is missed. The message tells you which criterion failed. The report calls this Failed.
No standardCalculated without a target, so there is nothing to pass or fail.

Calculation status

Awaiting runNo calculation has been run yet for this solution.
RecalculateYou changed the geometry, fixtures, workplanes or physics since the last run. Press Calculate Room to refresh the results.
CalculatedThe results match the current design.

Shortcuts worth knowing

EnterRuns Calculate Room when your cursor is not inside a text field.
EscCancels drawing or measuring, clears the selection and closes dialogs.
Ctrl+Z / Cmd+ZUndoes the last edit. Redo with Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z.
Space + dragPans the canvas without leaving Select mode.
Delete / BackspaceDeletes the selected fixture, pattern or workplane.

The letters (R), (F), (W), (L), (V) and (S) in the tool rail tooltips are labels only. Click a tool icon to open its panel.

If something goes wrong

Calculate Room is disabledHover over it to read the reason. A room needs at least 3 vertices, one luminaire and one workplane, and every height must fit under the ceiling.
An imported project shows Awaiting RunA .lxsc file does not keep old results. Open the Calculate Room menu and choose Calculate Entire Project.
A room is missing from the reportOnly calculated, up-to-date solutions appear. Click Calculate All when Studio asks.
A CAD room is the wrong sizeClick New in the CAD viewer and upload the drawing again with the correct source unit.

Not sure where to start? Click WIZARD. It asks about your room, the standard and the heights, then builds the study with you.
