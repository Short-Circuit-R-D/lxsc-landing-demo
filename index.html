From first room to finished report

The Quickstart wizard asks one thing at a time, so a first study never starts from a blank screen.

Step 1

Bring your plan or draw the room

Import a .dxf or .dwg drawing and Studio detects the rooms for you. Split open spaces, merge rooms and pick the source unit. No drawing? Sketch the room on the canvas or type its coordinates.

Step 2

Set the workplane and the target

Pick an EN 12464-1 activity and its illuminance and uniformity targets fill in, or enter your own. Then set the task height, wall zone, surface reflectances and maintenance factor.

Step 3

Add luminaires

Choose from the SC Catalog or upload your own .ies or .ldt file. Place fixtures one by one, draw a grid, or let Automate test layouts and return up to five ranked solutions.

Step 4

Calculate, compare, report

Press Calculate Room to see average, minimum and maximum illuminance, uniformity and power density with a clear compliance verdict. Compare solutions side by side, then export a PDF.

Catalog or custom photometry

Browse the SC Catalog by application, or upload an .ies (IES LM-63) or .ldt (EULUMDAT) file. LDT files are converted to IES automatically. Every fixture shows its POLAR WEB (C0/C90) diagram and peak intensity, so you can judge the distribution before you rely on it.

Place single fixtures or draw Grid, Staggered, Boundary and Linear patterns, then fine-tune mounting height, rotation and tilt. Save the project as one .lxsc file: your custom photometry travels with it, and you recalculate when you reopen it.
