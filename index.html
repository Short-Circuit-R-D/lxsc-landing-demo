Four steps from plan to report

Studio asks one thing at a time, so a first study never starts from a blank screen.

Step 1

Bring your plan, or type the room

Upload a DWG or DXF, let Studio detect the rooms and confirm the unit scale. No drawing? Enter the shape and dimensions by hand.

Step 2

Follow the standard, or set your own

Pick the standard and the targets fill in for you. Designing to your own brief? Set the targets yourself.

Step 3

Set heights for every fixture

Give the ceiling height and the mounting height for each fixture, and place them by hand or let the engine propose a layout.

Step 4

Review, calculate, report

Edit any step, run the calculation, and read the result on the page. Export the same report as a PDF.

Use your own fixtures

Upload an IES or LDT file and inspect its polar curves before you place a single luminaire: the intensity at every angle from straight down, and the beam angle where it falls to half of its peak. LDT files convert to IES for you.

Save the whole study as one .lxsc file. Every IES you used travels inside it, so it reopens on any machine exactly as you left it.
