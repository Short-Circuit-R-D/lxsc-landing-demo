A lighting design workspace in your browser

LuxScale Studio takes a room from floor plan to a standards-checked, client-ready report, whether it is a first office layout or a multi-room CAD project.

What LuxScale Studio is

LuxScale Studio is a web-based lighting calculation and design workspace. You draw or import your rooms on a metric 2D CAD canvas, place luminaires from a catalog or from your own photometric files, and calculate illuminance on task workplanes.

Results are checked live against EN 12464-1 or your own targets, so you always see whether a layout passes, is over-designed or falls short. When the design is ready, Studio assembles the numbers, polar diagrams, layout coordinates and your own images into a PDF report.

What you can do with it

Start fastThe Quickstart wizard sets up the room, workplane, target and physics in five steps, and can run an automated study for you.
Work from real drawingsImport .dxf or .dwg plans, split or merge rooms and bring the right boundary onto the canvas.
Design with precisionSnap, measure, undo and redo on a metric canvas, with live distance guides to the walls and to neighboring fixtures.
Compare alternativesKeep several rooms in one project and test multiple solutions side by side, by hand or with Automate.
See the lightFalse-color maps and a live lux probe show where the light falls on each workplane.
Deliver professionallyBuild the report with your logo, cover image, description and signature, then export a vector PDF.
