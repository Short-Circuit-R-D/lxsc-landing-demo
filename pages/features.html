What LuxScale Studio does

From a plan to a verified report, in one web app.

Bring any plan

Import a DWG or DXF, detect the rooms and confirm the unit scale, or draw the room by hand.

Standards built in

Pick the EN 12464-1 activity and the illuminance and uniformity targets fill in for you.

Your own fixtures

Upload IES or LDT files, convert LDT to IES, and inspect the polar curves before you place a luminaire.

Automatic layouts

Let the engine propose a fixture layout from the room dimensions, or place every luminaire yourself.

Reports that hold up

Compare solutions side by side, judge each task plane on its own, add photos and export a PDF.

Portable studies

Save a study as one .lxsc file with every fixture file inside.

Verifiable reports

Anyone can check a public report by its project ID in seconds, while private reports stay protected.
