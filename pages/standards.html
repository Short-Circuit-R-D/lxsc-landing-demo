Checked against EN 12464-1

LuxScale compares every task plane with the requirement for its activity, so you get a verdict, not just numbers.

What the check covers

EN 12464-1 sets the lighting requirements for indoor workplaces. In Studio you pick the activity, and the targets for maintained illuminance and uniformity fill in for you.

How a study is judged

Each task plane on its ownA plane that falls short is never hidden behind a good average from another plane.
Boundary marginTask planes are measured inside a margin you set, so the edges of the room do not skew the result.
Overdesign capStudio also flags layouts that use far more light than the target needs.
Power densityConnected load per square metre is reported next to the lighting result.

Your own targets

Not every project follows a standard. Set your own illuminance and uniformity targets, or calculate without targets and read the numbers directly.

LuxScale is a calculation tool. The designer remains responsible for the final design and for any additional requirements of the project.
